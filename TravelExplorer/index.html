About Travel Explorer

Travel Explorer is a seasonal travel planning website designed to help travelers discover the best destinations in Tamil Nadu based on the time of the year. Instead of randomly selecting destinations, the platform recommends places according to seasonal conditions, visitor crowd levels and travel suitability.

🌦 Season Based Travel

Find destinations that are best suited for the current season.

🔎 Smart Exploration

Search and filter destinations based on categories such as hillstations, waterfalls and heritage sites.

📍 Detailed Destination Guide

Each destination provides useful travel information including weather,crowd level, best visiting time and travel tips.
